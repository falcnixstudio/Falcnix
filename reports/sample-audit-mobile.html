Why speed matters to your revenue

Google & Deloitte research (“Milliseconds Make Millions”, 2020) found that improving mobile load time by 0.1 s lifted retail conversions by up to 8.4% and average order value by up to 9.2%. Core Web Vitals are also an official Google page-experience signal, so slow pages can lose rankings — which means losing organic traffic on top of lost conversions.
